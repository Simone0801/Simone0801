## Featured Portfolio Projects

### 1. [E-Commerce Customer Churn & RFM Segmentation](https://github.com/Simone0801/customer-churn-analysis)
> **Tech Stack:** `SQL (SQLite)` • `Power BI` • `DAX` • `RFM Modeling`
* **Objective:** Audit and segment 5,800+ customer accounts across 1.06M transaction records to identify silent attrition and quantify revenue at risk.
* **Technical Highlights:** Cleaned raw records with SQLite CTEs and window functions (`NTILE(4)`); engineered 13 custom DAX measures in Power BI with an interactive RFM matrix canvas.
* **Key Business Finding:** Quantified a **50.85% churn rate** (>90-day inactivity benchmark) and uncovered **£2.73M in recoverable revenue** concentrated in lapsed repeat buyers ("At Risk"), while 13% VIP accounts drove 54% of sales.

### 2. [Macroeconomic Impact of Mega Football Tournaments](https://github.com/Simone0801/football-events-economic-analysis)
> **Tech Stack:** `Python` • `Pandas` • `Statsmodels` • `Plotly`
* **Objective:** Construct an empirical panel study across **100 countries over 25 years (1996–2020)** to determine whether hosting FIFA, UEFA, and Copa América tournaments generates lasting economic growth.
* **Technical Highlights:** Harmonized World Bank and ILO macroeconomic indicators; implemented dynamic event-time alignment $[-3, +3]$ and estimated **Country Fixed Effects Difference-in-Differences (DiD)** regressions.
* **Key Finding:** Tournaments deliver only a short-lived anticipation surge in tourism (+4.3% at $t=-1$), but **fail to produce sustained economic gains**, with host real GDP growth decelerating by **1.65%** post-event ($p < 0.001$).

### 3. [Retail Commercial & Margin Performance Analysis](https://github.com/Simone0801/excel-superstore-sales-analysis)
> **Tech Stack:** `Microsoft Excel` • `Power Query (ETL)` • `PivotTables` • `Financial Modeling`
* **Objective:** Uncover profit leaks, evaluate discounting elasticity, and analyze category performance across 9,994 retail transactions.
* **Technical Highlights:** Standardized data types and engineered custom duration/margin features via Power Query; built an interactive 11-PivotTable executive BI dashboard with cross-filtering slicers.
* **Key Business Finding:** Transactions with **discounts >20% destroyed operating profitability**, driving **-$135K in cumulative losses**; Tables and Bookcases accounted for **95% of total product profit drag (-$21.2K)**.
