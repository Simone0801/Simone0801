## Featured Portfolio Projects

These are project that I developed:

### 1. [E-Commerce Customer Churn & RFM Segmentation](https://github.com/Simone0801/customer-churn-analysis)
> **Tech Stack:** `SQL (SQLite)` • `Power BI` • `DAX` • `RFM Modeling`
* **Objective:** Clean, aggregate, and segment 5,878 customer accounts across 1.06M transaction records to diagnose silent attrition and quantify historical gross spend exposure.
* **Technical Highlights:** Implemented multi-stage SQL cleaning and `NTILE(4)` quartile scoring with deterministic tie-breaking across 779k transaction rows; engineered 20 custom DAX measures with a dynamic, slicer-responsive RFM matrix in Power BI.
* **Key Business Finding:** Quantified a **50.85% churn rate** (>90-day retail inactivity benchmark) and identified **£2.73M in historical gross spend exposure** concentrated in 1,701 lapsed repeat buyers ("At Risk"), while 12.9% VIP Champions drove **54.4% (£9.44M)** of total gross value.

### 2. [Retail Commercial & Margin Performance Analysis](https://github.com/Simone0801/excel-superstore-sales-analysis)
> **Tech Stack:** `Microsoft Excel` • `Power Query (ETL)` • `PivotTables` • `Financial Modeling`
* **Objective:** Uncover profit leaks, evaluate discounting elasticity, and analyze category performance across 9,994 retail transactions.
* **Technical Highlights:** Standardized data types and engineered custom duration/margin features via Power Query; built an interactive 11-PivotTable executive BI dashboard with diverging subcategory bar charts, dual-axis commercial trends, and cross-filtering slicers.
* **Key Business Finding:** Orders with **discounts >20% compressed margins into steep deficits**, driving **-$135K in cumulative losses**; Tables and Bookcases accounted for **95% of total loss-making product drag (-$21.2K)**.
